Patient Management & EHR

Maintain centralized patient records with medical history, encounters, vital signs, prescriptions and diagnostics.

  • Patient Registration & MRNs
  • Unified Clinical Timeline
  • Recorded Vital Signs

Appointments & Scheduling

Coordinate patient consultations and doctor agendas through flexible table and schedule views.

  • In-page Booking Modal
  • Doctor Agendas
  • Daily Schedule Grid

Pharmacy & Medication

Manage medication inventory, prescriptions and dispensing from one connected pharmacy workspace.

  • Medication Catalog
  • Low-Stock Detection
  • Expiry Date Alerts

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Manage diagnostic test catalogs, patient lab orders and clinical results with status tracking.

  • Test Catalog & Ranges
  • Result Entry Modals
  • STAT Urgent Flags

Inpatient Wards & Beds

Monitor ward occupancy and manage patient admissions, transfers and discharges with bed visibility.

  • Bed Allocation Grid
  • Ward Occupancy Rate
  • Admissions & Transfers

Emergency & Triage

Support rapid emergency intake using structured ESI triage levels, vital signs and bay allocation.

  • Rapid ER Intake Modal
  • ESI Levels 1–5 Priority
  • ER Bay Assignments

Billing & Payments

Track patient invoices, payment receipts, outstanding balances and financial billing statements.

  • Patient Statements
  • Payment Receipts
  • Balance Tracking
